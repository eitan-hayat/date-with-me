Your WhatsApp Optional

Type it however you like — 052-123-4567 or +972 52 123 4567 both work. Leave it empty and she picks the chat herself when she taps Send it to you.
972 is Israel. Only used when the number has no code of its own.

How you pick her up Optional

Near the end she gets asked "How do you want me to pick you up?" and chooses from what is actually parked outside. Tap a tile to add the real photo of that bike or car — the exact model, not a stock picture. Rename anything, add more, or remove a row to drop it from her list.

Photos are shrunk to a wide thumbnail here in the browser before they go into the link. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

What she can choose from

Every option is on her list. Tap once to star it, tap again to take it off her list completely, tap a third time to put it back to normal.

When you're free

Two ways to do this. Either leave it open and just block the weekdays you never can, or pick the exact dates you're free and she gets only those.
